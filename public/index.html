The Turkish Boz Shepherd

An ancient guardian, bred for the work.

For thousands of years, Boz Shepherds have lived among the flocks of southeastern Turkey, standing between livestock and the wolves and bears that hunt them.

Calm, loyal, and independent, they are working guardians first — and devoted family dogs because of it.
