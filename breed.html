The Breed

The Boz Shepherd

The Boz Shepherd — known in Turkey as the Boz Çoban Köpeği — is an ancient livestock guardian breed from the Boz Shepherd homeland. For more than three thousand years, these dogs have walked beside Turkish shepherds, protecting flocks of sheep and goats from wolves, bears, jackals, and human predators across some of the harshest terrain on earth.

The name "Boz" means grey in Turkish — a nod to the breed's traditional brindle and grey coloring, often paired with a striking black mask. They are large, powerfully built, and remarkably athletic, combining the heavy bone of a mastiff with the agility of a true working shepherd.

Boz Shepherds are deeply bonded to their family and their flock — steady and affectionate at home, and immediate and decisive the moment something threatens what they are responsible for.

Temperament

What makes a Boz Shepherd a Boz Shepherd

Guardian Instinct

Boz Shepherds have been selectively bred for thousands of years to bond with and defend livestock. They patrol their territory, position themselves between threats and their charges, and escalate force only when needed.

Independent Thinker

Unlike herding breeds that take constant direction, Boz Shepherds make their own decisions in the field. This intelligence and self-reliance is a strength — but it requires an owner who respects it.

Family Devotion

At home they are quiet, patient, and deeply attached to their people. A well-raised Boz Shepherd is gentle with the children of the household and settles easily indoors — while never losing track of who belongs and who does not.

Built for Work

Heavy bone, deep chest, powerful jaws, and tireless endurance. Boz Shepherds can cover rough terrain for hours and stand their ground against wolves, bears, and large predators.

Did You Know

Remarkable facts about the Boz Shepherd

743 PSI

The strongest bite on earth

The Boz Shepherd is widely credited with the strongest measured bite force of any domestic dog — powerful enough to deter wolves, big cats, and bears.

Bear-capable

Built to face apex predators

A mature Boz Shepherd is one of the very few dogs in the world physically and mentally capable of taking on a bear single-handedly to protect its flock and family.

World record

Lineage of the tallest Boz Shepherd ever

Our line traces directly back to Minik — the Boz Shepherd that holds the world record as the tallest Boz Shepherd in the world. Ruby and Nala are direct descendants of Minik through their sire's side.

30+ mph

Surprisingly fast for their size

Despite weighing 150–190 lbs, a Boz Shepherd can hit speeds over 30 mph in a sprint — combining mastiff power with true working-shepherd athleticism.

Is a Boz Shepherd right for you?

A serious dog for the right home.

Boz Shepherds thrive on working farms, ranches, and secure rural properties where they have space to patrol and a job to do. They do best with experienced owners who understand large guardian breeds and can provide consistent leadership, secure fencing, and early socialization.

They are not apartment dogs and they are not for casual first-time owners. But for the right family or rancher, a well-raised Boz Shepherd is one of the most rewarding dogs you will ever own.
