A few honest expectations.

These dogs grow into 110–190 lb guardians that make their own decisions. They need secure fencing, early socialization, and an owner who leads consistently without trying to drill the independence out of them.

If that does not describe your situation, we will tell you — and we would rather do that now than place a dog badly.
